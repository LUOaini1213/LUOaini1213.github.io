malaysia-auto-ask · Malaysia auto Q&A

SQLite · fixed SQL templates · 30+8 eval cases

SQLlineageHITLEval
  • Why When business asks who sells well, some mean association wholesale (TIV) and some mean registrations — two numbers that do not match. First tell which set is being asked, then produce the table, so one sentence does not get two answers.
  • What A sentence comes in; it only answers wholesale, registrations, or share that are already defined. A frontline page shows year sales cards; a note for colleagues copies only after a confirm checkbox.
  • How If wholesale vs registrations is unclear, or the question is “last month”, “luxury cars”, or a year not in the database, it stops and asks a person — it does not guess a number. Self-test: 30 questions; 22 that should return numbers are all correct; 8 stop (26.7%).

2026 NUS · CE5001

Flooded multimodal networks: DEM, SUMO, LLM agents

Course project · report PDF

SUMOLLMEval
  • Situation Bus and car routing under flood. Network, demand and inundation were not on one map; a larger model is not automatically the answer on dense vs sparse streets.
  • Task Build a comparable evaluation pipeline; simulate bus and car separately; use experiments to decide when rules suffice and when to use a model.
  • Action ~3,500 lines / 10 modules. Rules, persona-augmented rules, a local small model, and a cloud model — 200 paired runs. Bus data: 793 routes, 5,201 stops.
  • Result In a dense CBD, rule agents cut related travel time 62.2%; on sparse nets, the model reached similar impact with far fewer actions. Report PDF on this site.
